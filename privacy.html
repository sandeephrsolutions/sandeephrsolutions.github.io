Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

Sandeep Solutions respects your privacy and explains below what personal information we may receive, why we use it, where it may be stored or shared, and how you can contact us about your information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may receive:

  • Name, mobile number, email address and other contact details.
  • Educational qualifications, professional experience and current designation.
  • Current and expected salary information, preferred location, notice period and key skills.
  • Resume or LinkedIn profile information supplied by a candidate.
  • Employer/company details, vacancy information and job requirements supplied by employers or clients.
  • Information contained in enquiries or communications that you voluntarily send to us.

2. How We Receive Information

Information may be submitted through the website, the Candidate Portal, employer or contact forms, email, WhatsApp, telephone or other direct business communications.

3. Why We Use Information

  • Recruitment, candidate sourcing and matching candidates with relevant opportunities.
  • Responding to employer, candidate and service enquiries.
  • Providing HR & recruitment, business consultancy, restaurant solutions, documentation & setup, and related business-support services.
  • Communicating about vacancies, enquiries, services and business matters.
  • Maintaining appropriate business, recruitment and service records.
  • Meeting applicable legal, compliance and record-keeping requirements where relevant.

4. Candidate Portal Storage

Candidate account information submitted through the Candidate Portal is handled using the Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore services configured for Sandeep Solutions. These services are third-party technology providers and may process or store information as part of providing their services.

5. WhatsApp, Email & Other Providers

When a website form opens WhatsApp, the information you choose to send is transmitted through WhatsApp. Information sent by email, social media or other third-party communication services may remain with those providers under their respective terms and privacy policies.

Important: Please share only information that is relevant to your recruitment, employment or business enquiry. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identity numbers or other information that is not required for the particular enquiry.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Candidate information may be shared with prospective employers or relevant clients when appropriate for recruitment and where the information is relevant to the opportunity. We may also use service providers or communication platforms needed to operate our website and business channels.

7. Consent and Your Choices

Where we ask you to provide consent for a particular use of personal information, you may choose whether to provide it. You may also contact us to withdraw consent for future processing where withdrawal is applicable. Withdrawal does not necessarily affect processing already carried out or information that we need to retain for legal, compliance or record-keeping purposes.

8. Access, Correction & Deletion

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have submitted to us, subject to applicable legal, compliance and record-keeping requirements. Requests relating to a candidate account may require reasonable verification of the account holder.

9. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including recruitment, business communication, contractual, legal, compliance and record-keeping needs. When information is no longer required and there is no applicable reason to retain it, we may delete or anonymize it where reasonably practicable.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information received through our business channels. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Access to candidate account information is controlled through the configured candidate authentication and database rules.

11. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use basic browser technologies required for normal website operation. If we introduce non-essential analytics, advertising or similar tracking technologies, we will review the relevant notice and consent requirements and update this policy as appropriate.

12. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites and platforms such as LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party services.

13. Applicable Privacy Requirements

This policy describes our current information-handling practices and should be read together with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, to the extent applicable to our activities.
