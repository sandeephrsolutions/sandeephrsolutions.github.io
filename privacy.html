Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Sandeep HR Solutions (also presented on this website as Sandeep Solutions) respects the privacy of candidates, employers and website visitors. This notice explains the types of information that may be collected when you use our website or contact us.

Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the website, information may include your name, email address, phone number, employer or company details, job preferences, qualifications, work experience, CV or resume link, application details, and messages you choose to submit. Candidate account functions may also process authentication and profile information.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, provide recruitment and HR services, manage candidate accounts and job applications, communicate about relevant vacancies, maintain website security, and meet applicable legal or business obligations.

Recruitment and sharing

Where relevant to a recruitment opportunity, candidate information may be shared with a prospective employer for consideration. We aim to share only information relevant to that opportunity. Please avoid submitting information that is not needed for your enquiry or application. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers and storage

Candidate authentication and certain candidate, job and enquiry records may be processed using Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. When you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, email or another external service, that service processes information under its own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, including recruitment follow-up, recordkeeping, dispute resolution and legal obligations. We use access controls and other reasonable safeguards, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with your enquiry or candidate account, request correction, or ask for deletion where applicable. We may need to verify your identity and may retain some records where required or permitted by law.

Children and external links

This website is intended for people seeking employment or business services, not for children. The website may link to third-party services; we do not control their privacy practices.

Policy updates

We may update this notice when our website or services change. The latest revision date will be shown on this page.
