Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Sandeep HR Solutions respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information shared with us through our website, recruitment services, enquiry forms and communication channels.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the service you use, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact details
  • Mobile number and email address
  • Educational qualifications
  • Professional experience and current designation
  • Current and expected salary information
  • Preferred job location
  • Notice period and key skills
  • Resume or LinkedIn profile information
  • Company and job requirement details from employers

2. How We Use Information

Your information may be used for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Recruitment and candidate sourcing
  • Matching candidates with suitable job opportunities
  • Responding to employer enquiries
  • Providing HR consulting and recruitment services
  • Communicating regarding enquiries, vacancies and services
  • Maintaining business and service records

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Candidate information may be shared with prospective employers or relevant clients when necessary for recruitment purposes and where appropriate to the recruitment process.

4. WhatsApp Communication

Website enquiry and candidate registration forms may open WhatsApp to submit information to Sandeep HR Solutions. WhatsApp is a third-party communication service and its own privacy policies may apply.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information received through our business channels. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for recruitment, business communication, legal, compliance or record-keeping purposes.

7. Your Choices

You may contact us to request correction of inaccurate information or to ask about the handling of information you have submitted to us.
