Module 01 · Beginner

HR & Recruitment Fundamentals

Recruitment is a structured process to understand a vacancy, attract suitable applicants, assess skills and help the selected candidate join successfully.

Typical recruitment workflow

  1. Confirm the job description, must-have skills, location, shift and salary range with the hiring manager.
  2. Choose sourcing channels and prepare a clear job advertisement.
  3. Screen applications against job-related criteria and document decisions consistently.
  4. Coordinate interviews, collect interviewer feedback and communicate next steps.
  5. Verify approved documents, issue the offer through the authorised process and coordinate onboarding.
  6. Track joining, candidate experience and recruitment metrics for improvement.
Practical tip: Keep candidate data confidential, collect only what is needed, and use consistent job-related selection criteria.

Practice task

Draft a five-point screening checklist for a Customer Service Executive role. Include communication, relevant experience, shift availability, location and salary expectations.

Frequently asked questions

Before you start

Are the materials downloadable?

The planned experience is browser-based reading, not public download links. However, web content cannot be made completely impossible to copy; access controls and viewer restrictions only reduce casual downloading.

Are all books available now?

Not yet. The page provides the Learning Hub structure and starter lessons. Approved PDF books and additional course materials must be added separately.

Can beginners use it?

Yes. The learning tracks are designed to support beginners and working professionals. Each course can be expanded with beginner, intermediate and practical modules.

Does the Learning Hub replace professional advice?

No. Legal, tax, statutory registration and compliance topics are educational summaries. Users should verify current official requirements or consult a qualified professional for case-specific advice.
