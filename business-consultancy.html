WHAT MAY BE INCLUDED

Typical deliverables

Deliverables are finalized according to the requirement and written quotation.

  • Initial requirement and challenge discussion
  • Current-state process review
  • Priority and gap identification
  • Practical action plan with ownership
  • SOP or checklist recommendations where agreed
  • Implementation milestones and review points
  • Progress review and improvement recommendations
  • Written scope and quotation before work begins

Engagement principles

Clear scope: work starts after the deliverables, responsibilities and commercial terms are agreed.

Transparent communication: we clarify required information, dependencies and progress updates.

Realistic expectations: hiring decisions, business outcomes and government approvals can depend on factors outside the consultancy's control.

Confidentiality: sensitive business and candidate information should be shared only through appropriate channels and handled with care.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you get started

Who can use the consultancy service?

Startups, small businesses and growing organizations that need practical support with operations, process or planning.

Do you provide a fixed package for every business?

The scope is tailored after understanding the requirement. A quotation can define deliverables, timelines and fees.

Will you implement recommendations?

Implementation support can be included where agreed in the written scope; advisory-only engagements are also possible.

Can results be guaranteed?

Business results depend on multiple factors, including execution and market conditions. Recommendations are practical guidance, not a guarantee of financial outcomes.

Let's discuss your business challenge.

Tell us about your operating challenge, desired outcome and timeline. We will help define a practical scope of support.
