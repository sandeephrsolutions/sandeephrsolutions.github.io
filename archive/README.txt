Sandeep HR Solutions — Step 4.4 Legal & Compliance Review
Date: October 3, 2026

Purpose
This package provides website wording updates/checklists for the legal and compliance items in the website review report. It is not legal advice or legal certification.

IMPORTANT
- Do not claim that the website/business holds a placement-agency licence unless the business has verified and can document that claim.
- Do not invent employer service fees, refund terms, statutory registrations, licences or certifications.
- Candidate recruitment/job-registration services are stated as NO FEE.
- Pan-India / All India is the service coverage. Bengaluru / Electronic City is business-location context only.
- Privacy wording should match the actual Firebase Authentication + Cloud Firestore implementation and WhatsApp/email workflows.
- Any final liability, jurisdiction, employment-law or regulatory wording should be reviewed by a qualified professional where appropriate.

Current review findings
1. Privacy Policy: substantially covers collection, purposes, Firebase Authentication/Firestore, WhatsApp/email, sharing, consent, access/correction/deletion, retention, security, cookies/analytics, external links and DPDP Act wording.
2. Terms: already contains candidate NO FEE, candidate account responsibilities, employer/client responsibilities, commercial-terms language, third-party services, privacy, India-law wording and a legal-review note.
3. Disclaimer: contains NO FEE and recruitment/no-guarantee wording, but should be brought into the standardized site shell and updated to the same date.
4. Terms footer currently says Bengaluru • Electronic City as “Service Area”; change this to “Pan-India / All India”.
5. Disclaimer currently has an older update date (September 30, 2026); align it to October 3, 2026 after review.
6. Keep the Udyam number only as an MSME registration statement; do not describe it as a placement licence.
7. Keep candidate consent before WhatsApp submission and link to Privacy Policy/Terms from candidate-facing forms.
8. Employer enquiries may be publicly creatable because the current employer form is designed to accept submissions without an employer account; admin read/update/delete remains restricted by Firestore rules.
9. The site should not promise employment, placement, interview, salary or commercial outcomes.
10. Final publication should be followed by a manual review of all legal pages and a live-site check.
