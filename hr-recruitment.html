WHAT MAY BE INCLUDED

Typical deliverables

  • Role and requirement discussion
  • Job description / role profile alignment
  • Candidate sourcing and initial screening
  • Shortlist sharing and interview coordination
  • Candidate communication and follow-up
  • Offer and joining coordination
  • Hiring status updates
  • Optional onboarding documentation support

Engagement principles

Clear scope: work starts after the deliverables, responsibilities and commercial terms are agreed.

Transparent communication: we clarify required information, dependencies and progress updates.

Realistic expectations: hiring decisions, business outcomes and government approvals can depend on factors outside the consultancy's control.

Confidentiality: sensitive business and candidate information should be shared only through appropriate channels and handled with care.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you get started

What roles can you recruit for?

Recruitment can cover junior, mid-level, senior, bulk and specialist positions, subject to role requirements and agreed scope.

Can you support urgent or bulk hiring?

Yes. Share the number of openings, job profiles, locations and expected timeline so a practical hiring plan can be discussed.

How are recruitment charges decided?

Fees depend on role level, hiring volume, service scope and agreed commercial terms. A written quotation is provided before engagement.

Do you guarantee a joining date?

Hiring outcomes depend on candidate availability, interview decisions, notice periods and employer approvals. Timelines are planned transparently, not guaranteed.

Ready to discuss your hiring requirement?

Share your vacancies, job descriptions, hiring volume and target timeline. We will discuss the suitable recruitment approach and commercial scope.
